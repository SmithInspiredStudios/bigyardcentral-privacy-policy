Big Yard Central AI Agent Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026 | Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. About this application

Big Yard Central AI Agent is a private tool used only by Big Yard Central to manage its own eBay seller account: listings, sales analysis, marketing, and customer service. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect information from visitors to this page.

2. Information we access

With the account owner’s authorization through eBay’s official sign-in process, the tool accesses listings, inventory, offers, sales analytics, order and shipping details, buyer messages, and return or cancellation information. This can include buyers’ names, shipping addresses, and message contents. The tool never sees or stores the owner’s eBay password and does not access payment card or bank account details. It requests only the permissions needed for the features in use.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to research products, analyze sales, improve listings, prepare marketing recommendations, review orders, and draft customer service replies. It is not used for any unrelated purpose.

The tool does not edit listings, send messages, issue refunds, accept or decline offers, cancel orders, or make any other change to the eBay account without the owner’s explicit approval of that specific action.

4. Artificial intelligence services

The tool uses OpenRouter and the AI models available through it to process requests. Only the information needed for a task is sent. Each provider’s handling of data, including retention and any use for model training, is governed by its own policies, which we do not control.

5. Storage and security

Credentials and business data are stored on the account owner’s computer. Access is limited to the account owner. If a security incident affects buyer information, we will notify affected parties and eBay as required by law. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent information, and we do not use it for advertising. Information is shared only with eBay, with the AI providers described above, and where required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

Information is kept only as long as needed for business purposes and legal obligations, and we will delete personal information on request where permitted by law. The owner can revoke the tool’s access at any time in eBay account settings. Revoking access does not delete information already processed by third-party providers.

8. Your requests

Anyone whose information may have been handled through this tool can contact us to ask what we hold or to request correction or deletion.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the tool changes. The current version and its date will always be posted at this URL.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: bigyardcapital@gmail.com
